`pxr/usd/usdGeom/testenv/testUsdGeomBBoxCache.py`, copied unchanged from
`pxr/usd/usdGeom/testenv/testUsdGeomBBoxCache/` of
<https://github.com/PixarAnimationStudios/OpenUSD>. They are Copyright
Pixar, under the licence in [`third-party/`](../../third-party/LICENSE).

`nestedInstanceTest.usda` is the sublayer of the three `disable*.usda`
files.
